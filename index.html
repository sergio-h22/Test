How it runs

Four steps

The order below is the actual order things happen in. Nothing gets printed until you have signed off on the proof.

01

Send artwork

Email your file or describe what you want. No file yet? Tell us the idea and we will set it up.

02

Proof & quote

You get a digital proof and a firm price. Revisions here are free — this is the stage to change your mind.

03

We print

Approved proof goes to the press. You get a message the moment the run comes off.

04

Pick up or ship

Collect from the shop, or we box it and send it to you. Your call at checkout.
